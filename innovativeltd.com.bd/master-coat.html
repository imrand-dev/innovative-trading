A brand which has been leading for more than last 20 years has become the generic name for exterior paints, the name is none other than ‘Master Coat’. Since its launch, the product has gone through a series of innovation to keep up with time. Till today, Master Coat is one of the most well-known brands in the Bangladesh paint industry.

Volatile Organic Compounds (VOCs). Master Coat has very LOW VOLATILE ORGANIC COMPOUNDS (VOCs), which does not harm either human or the environment. The emission of volatiles in large quantities is harmful to humans as well as the environment.

Master Coat ensures maximum coverage that is 20% extra than any other exterior paint in the South-East Asia. It is based on 100% ACRYLIC ACRYLIC EMULSION and has all the necessary properties to give exterior walls maximum protection, which can last up to 10 years under extreme weather conditions.

 

Master Coat is APEO (Alkylphenol Ethoxylates) Free paint. APEO is nonylphenol compounds. Nonylphenol Ethoxylates can enter into the body either by inhalation or by dermal contact.

However, the exposure to high level of Nonylphenol Ethoxylates may cause irritation of the lungs, skin, eyes and disorder of the digestive system. Nonylphenol Ethoxylates readily degrade the environment to more toxic. The International Agency for Research on Cancer has not designated nonylphenol ethoxylates in terms of its carcinogenicity.

MASTER COAT is also FORMALDEHYDE FREE paint.Formaldehyde is a colorless and strong-smelling gas often found in water-based solutions. It can be released into the air (off-gas) from materials and products made with it. It is also widely used as an industrial fungicide, germicide and disinfectant. Formaldehyde exposure may potentially cause a variety of symptoms and adverse health effects such as eye, nose, throat, and skin irritation, coughing, wheezing, and allergic reactions.

In addition, the long-term exposure to high level of formaldehyde has been associated with cancer.

ELITE PAINT is the 1st paint company in Bangladesh to achieve Lead Safe Paint® certification. Paints from Elite Paint were certified by a leading third-party certifier SCS Global Services (SCS), the program’s exclusive certification body in Bangladesh. The independent analysis of SCS confirmed that Elite Paint contained less than a total of 90 ppm lead.

Elite Paint is licensed to use the Lead Safe Paint® certification mark on its paint container labels and other promotional materials. Using this mark will provide consumers with confidence that these paints will protect their families from the hazard of lead exposure.

According to the World Health Organization (WHO), “lead paint is one of the largest sources of exposure to lead in children.” Lead exposure during early childhood years has been linked to an increased likelihood of impaired cognition and executive function, impulsiveness, aggression and delinquent behavior. Brain damage caused by chronic low-level exposure to lead is irreversible and untreatable.

So, reducing lead exposure is an important worldwide health issue.

Preparation for the site: Before applying paint, dust, oil, and fungus should be cleaned up properly from the exterior wall of the building. For a new wall, use 120 sandpaper or stone and for the old wall, use a scraper or soap to clean up. Wall should be dried up properly before applying paint to cure. For newly plastered wall, give about 12 weeks for curing.

First Step: Apply Master Coat Exterior Sealer or A close Sealer at the wall which will be dried up within 3 - 5 hours. If there is a chance of salt, then Salt Seal can be applied as an undercoat.

Second Step: For covering different flaws and ensuring smoothness, use Puttelite Wall Putty or Multipurpose Putty. It will take 3 - 4 hours to get the wall dried up. After that, smoothen the wall by using 220 sand paper.

Third Step: Thin up to 25% - 30% by adding clean water with Master Coat. For a smoother finish, 2 coats of paint can be applied. Extra 1 coat paint can be used for any deeper shade. On the sunshed, where there is a possibility of waterlogging, 3 coats of paint can be applied. Per litre coverage is about 160 sft - 180 sft.

Special Note: Stir the paint well before using. Use clean brush and roller for proper application. It is better not to use chalk powder for getting accurate color. Tightly close the lid after every uses so that air does not harm the paint. Clean all equipments with water immediately after uses. Mix 5% soap or detergent powder with water to remove stains from the wall. Use a thin material to clean up properly.
