About Us

modern & innovative wood coatings solutions

Innovative Trading, Our company is doing business since 2013. We currently supply our products in various parts of Bangladesh in many sectors such as architecture and constructions, modern and contemporary furniture industries, paint and coatings industries, printing industries, paints & hardware shops. The core business of Innovative Trading is to provide modern & innovative wood coatings solutions to the furniture's, architectural, constructions & retailers. Since more than a decades we are proudly working in various sectors with customer satisfactions. Besides wood coatings we are supplying raw materials & providing technical supports to the various sectors such as paint & coatings, thinners & packaging industries. We ensure best quality products, technical services & doorstep delivery facilities to customers & consumers.

Mission

To develop trusted client relationship by providing exceptional quality products & innovative modern finishes with protection to the furniture industries for acquire more space in global as well as domestic demands arena.

VISION

To be the prime as well as trusted modern wood coatings to furniture, finest quality raw materials provider for paint & coating industries .
